# Summarize Refund Policies Across Tabs

## Goal
Collect refund terms from several websites and summarize the differences.

## Example Prompt
Review the refund policy pages I have open and summarize the key terms, refund windows, exclusions, and whether each one offers a full refund.

## Task Type
- Research

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- tab management
- extraction
- navigation
- comparison

## Success Criteria
The agent identifies the important refund terms for each site and presents them in a structured comparison.

## Notes
Useful for ecommerce, SaaS, and subscription-service research.
