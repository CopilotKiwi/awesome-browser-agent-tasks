# Compare Pricing Across SaaS Sites

## Goal
Review several SaaS pricing pages and compare plan structure, pricing, and included features.

## Example Prompt
Open these SaaS pricing pages, compare the plans, and tell me which one looks best for a small team of five users.

## Task Type
- Comparison

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- tab management
- navigation
- extraction
- comparison

## Success Criteria
The agent returns a clear side-by-side comparison with prices, feature differences, and a recommendation.

## Notes
Works best when tabs are already open or when the target sites are easy to reach from their homepages.
