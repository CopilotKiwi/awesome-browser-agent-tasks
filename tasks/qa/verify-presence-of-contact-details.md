# Verify Presence Of Contact Details

## Goal
Check whether important pages clearly show contact details.

## Example Prompt
Review these pages and verify whether visible contact details are present, including email, phone, address, or contact form links.

## Task Type
- QA

## Difficulty
- Easy

## Skills Required
- extraction
- navigation

## Success Criteria
The agent reports which pages do or do not show clear contact information.

## Notes
Useful for website audits and business-directory checks.
