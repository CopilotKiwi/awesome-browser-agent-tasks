# Compare Service Providers

## Goal
Compare a shortlist of service providers based on website information.

## Example Prompt
Compare these service providers based on pricing signals, offering, industries served, and support or trust signals, then recommend the strongest option.

## Task Type
- Comparison

## Difficulty
- Hard

## Skills Required
- comparison
- tab management
- extraction
- navigation

## Success Criteria
The agent produces a useful comparison with enough evidence to support a recommendation.

## Notes
This works well for agencies, software vendors, and outsourced service providers.
