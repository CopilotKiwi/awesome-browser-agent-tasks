# Collect Procurement Details From Vendor Pages

## Goal
Extract procurement-relevant information from vendor websites.

## Example Prompt
Review these vendor pages and collect the contact method, service coverage, onboarding process, certifications, and any pricing or minimum commitment details shown.

## Task Type
- Extraction

## Difficulty
- Hard

## Skills Required
- extraction
- navigation
- scrolling

## Success Criteria
The agent returns structured procurement notes for each vendor.

## Notes
Good for purchasing research and supplier shortlist building.
