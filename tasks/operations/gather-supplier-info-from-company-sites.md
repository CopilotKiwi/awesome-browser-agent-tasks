# Gather Supplier Info From Company Sites

## Goal
Collect supplier details from multiple company websites for sourcing or procurement work.

## Example Prompt
Review these supplier websites and extract the supplier name, product or service focus, location, contact details, and any procurement-related information shown.

## Task Type
- Extraction

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- navigation
- extraction
- scrolling

## Success Criteria
The agent returns supplier profiles with the requested fields in a consistent structure.

## Notes
Often requires checking homepage, about, and contact pages together.
