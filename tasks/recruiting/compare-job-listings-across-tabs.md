# Compare Job Listings Across Tabs

## Goal
Compare job opportunities from multiple listing pages or company careers pages.

## Example Prompt
Compare these job listings and summarize the role differences, location, salary details if visible, and application requirements.

## Task Type
- Comparison

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- tab management
- extraction
- comparison

## Success Criteria
The agent returns a clean comparison that helps a user quickly assess the options.

## Notes
Useful for recruiting research or job search automation.
