# Compare Product Pricing Across Stores

## Goal
Compare the price of the same or similar product across multiple online stores.

## Example Prompt
Compare the price of this product across the open store tabs and tell me which one is the best overall deal once shipping is considered.

## Task Type
- Comparison

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- tab management
- extraction
- comparison

## Success Criteria
The agent identifies prices, shipping impact, and returns a clear best-value summary.

## Notes
Useful for shopping assistants and sourcing workflows.
