# Enter Repeated Business Details Across Portals

## Goal
Enter the same business profile information across multiple vendor or partner portals.

## Example Prompt
Use this business profile and fill the repeated company details across the portal pages I open, then tell me which fields could not be completed.

## Task Type
- Multi-step workflow

## Difficulty
- Hard

## Skills Required
- form entry
- navigation
- clicking
- scrolling

## Success Criteria
The agent fills the repeated details consistently and reports any missing or incompatible fields.

## Notes
Portal flows often include multiple steps and inconsistent field labels.
