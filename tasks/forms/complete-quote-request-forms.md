# Complete Quote Request Forms

## Goal
Fill quote or enquiry forms with structured business information and requirements.

## Example Prompt
Complete these quote request forms using our company details and the service requirements listed below.

## Task Type
- Form filling

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- form entry
- clicking
- navigation

## Success Criteria
The relevant company fields, contact fields, and request details are entered accurately.

## Notes
Quote forms often include optional dropdowns, checkboxes, or free-text requirement fields.
