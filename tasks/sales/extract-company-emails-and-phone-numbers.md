# Extract Company Emails And Phone Numbers

## Goal
Find and extract direct contact details from company websites.

## Example Prompt
Review these company websites and extract the main contact email address and phone number for each one.

## Task Type
- Extraction

## Difficulty
- Easy

## Skills Required
- navigation
- extraction

## Success Criteria
The agent returns the company names with the best visible email and phone contact details.

## Notes
Useful for lead research, prospecting, and contact validation.
