# Gather Business Information From Company Websites

## Goal
Collect key business details from company websites for sales research.

## Example Prompt
Visit these company websites and gather the company name, main offering, target market, location, and contact details.

## Task Type
- Extraction

## Difficulty
- Medium

## Skills Required
- navigation
- extraction
- scrolling

## Success Criteria
The agent returns structured business summaries without obvious missing fields.

## Notes
This often requires combining homepage, about, and contact page information.
